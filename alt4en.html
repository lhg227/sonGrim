Four connected floors

4F Sky Garden → 3F Little Garden → 2F Small Ring → 1F Ground Floor. Drop through the central openings freely: there is no fall damage. The broad ground floor and igloo walls keep you within the map.

The mint-edged spiral stairs connect floors 1 through 4. Follow the green arrows. A blue jetpack works for 10 seconds from pickup. Jetpacks respawn on the ground and upper floors. Use WASD while flying.

Four subspaces

Walk through the east passage on each floor, shown on the right side of the map. 1F EUREKA Music Cafe: approach the three performers and they reveal their mafia identities. They have 425 HP and respawn after 20 seconds. A 3D display behind the stage shows three jumping cats and a baby mouse.
2F SKYFALL Giant Plaza contains two gun giants and two sword giants. 3F FOURTEEN Garden contains fourteen animal characters. Giants and garden animals have 425 HP, five times an ordinary enemy’s health. Their allegiance changes, but they stay in their own space until attacked by the player. Then they pursue you through passages and up the main stairs.
4F PIZZA Street has six hungry pizza zombies that chase nearby players. Deplete their 425 HP and they eat pizza, find peace, then respawn at home after 10 seconds.

Looting · 45 weapon combinations

Start with one random weapon and element. Approach a weapon on the ground to collect it automatically. A new pickup replaces the element in the same numbered slot. Empty slots cannot be used. Defeated enemies have a 60% chance to drop a weapon.
Blue / Wood: +30% move speed while held. Red / Fire: hits burn for 2 seconds, dealing 10% of the enemy’s maximum HP per second; burning enemies glow orange. Yellow / Earth: damage ×1.3 with downward recoil. White / Metal: damage ×1.3 with upward recoil. Black / Extra Shot: each shot has a 20% chance to fire an extra dark bullet. Projectiles and sentries retain the element they had when fired or deployed.

Press E within 6.75m of food vendors, delivery customers, or the upgrade merchant. This is 1.5 times the original interaction range, and works across counters and shop walls on the same floor.

The three-minute dinosaur

After 3 minutes of active play, the first green dinosaur appears on your current floor and pursues you. Pausing stops the clock. It uses a green version of the garden dragon, twice a knight’s width and height, with 1,275 HP and 30 damage per flame. The right panel shows its countdown and health. After defeating it, press E near the campfire at the center of 1F to receive 100 gold and summon another after 60 seconds. You can light the fire once per defeat. Each rematch multiplies health and fire damage by another 1.1. Rematch colors cycle Blue → Red → Yellow → White → Black. Defeat counts and summon countdowns appear on screen.

Destructible cover crates

Yellow and gray cover crates have 45 HP and can be destroyed by bullets, projectiles, flames, and explosions. Destroyed crates no longer block movement or shots and do not respawn. Building walls and doors cannot be destroyed.

Ranks · Weapon levels · V skill

Defeating an enemy grants 3 XP, matching its three dropped coins. XP is awarded immediately, without collecting the coins. Shop, delivery, and campfire rewards do not grant XP. Private through Sergeant require 10 XP per promotion; Staff Sergeant through Sergeant Major require 20; Second Lieutenant through Colonel require 30; Brigadier General through General require 40. The final rank is Super General.

Each numbered weapon slot levels up once per 10 combat XP, up to level 45. Normal and alternate modes share that slot’s level. Picking up another weapon with the same number does not reset its level. V underbarrel skills share a 4-second cooldown that persists when switching slots. X detonates your car.

The nine-level skill cycle is Masterkey → Rocket → Dragon’s Breath → Grenade → Slow Heavy Shield → One-second Stun Pulse → Fast Poison Arrow → Slow Explosive Arrow → Slow Stun Wave. Level 9 has the stun wave; level 10 starts again with a red Masterkey. Poison deals 8 base damage per second for 4 seconds. The stun wave also stops enemies for 1 second.

Levels 1–9, Blue: move +10%. Levels 10–18, Red: move +15%. Levels 19–27, Yellow: move +10%, V damage +10%. Levels 28–36, White: move +15%, V damage +10%. Levels 37–45, Black: move +15%, V damage +15%. Bonuses apply to the held slot and multiply with the original blue-element movement bonus.

Q · Alternate mode

Press Q to use the current slot’s alternate weapon. You cannot return to normal mode for the first 10 seconds; afterward, press Q again to return. Switching slots preserves the mode and lock timer. Element effects also apply in alternate mode. Press R to reload the MAC10 or AK47 manually.

Nine base weapons

1 Shotgun · 2 Rocket · 3 Flamethrower · 4 Grenade · 5 Minigun · 6 Sentry · 7 Medi Gun · 8 Sniper (hold and release for a charged shot) · 9 Silenced Pistol. Ammunition is unlimited. The pistol makes a soft shot sound and leaves a smoky trail that vanishes within a second. The Medi Gun heals you and your cats, and repairs nearby cars.

Enemy health and hit feedback

Rings around enemies’ feet change from green to yellow to red as their health drops. Hits produce pale-purple cubes and a soft sound. Ordinary enemies respawn at home after 8 seconds.

Friends, cars, and houses

Hire the golden, white, and black cats near Blue Base for 5 coins each. Every 3 seconds, each cat has a 25% chance to shoot the nearest enemy for 9 damage, equal to one minigun bullet. If it does not fire, it has a 50% chance to display one of ten cheers. Cats follow you between floors.

Press B to buy a car for 5 coins or enter a nearby one. Ramming damages enemies and the car. You cannot fire or use a jetpack while driving. Exit and repair it with weapon 7, the Medi Gun. If it is far away, detonate it with X and summon a replacement with B for 5 coins. Detonation emits magenta and orange cubes and deals half of each enemy’s maximum HP within 9m. It does not hurt you.

The four houses on 4F belong to Sheep (coins), Dino (levels), Baby Mouse (YA-HO! damage boost), and Squid (full healing and 5-second invincibility). Enter through their doors and press E to talk. Pay the merchant 10 coins with E to increase your movement level. Each level adds 0.35 to move speed and initial jump speed, up to 14 and 15 respectively. These movement levels do not increase attack damage or maximum HP.

Deliveries and the flag

1F Burrito → 4F Pig / 2F Burger → 4F Dachshund / 3F Army Stew → 4F Calf / 4F Pizza → 4F Chicken. Press E at the stand and customer to pick up and deliver food. Rewards are 10–20 coins.

Take the flag from the red base on 4F back to Blue Base. Then enter HOME behind Blue Base to finish the adventure. At zero HP, you lose 5 coins and respawn at Blue Base.

Progress is not saved automatically. Refreshing starts a new game. There are no mobile controls.
